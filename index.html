    How the calculation works

    Total pitch-minutes = players on pitch × match length. Each available player's fair share is that figure divided by the number of available players. With a fixed keeper, the keeper plays the whole match and the outfield minutes are shared among everyone else.

    The plan is built block by block: in each sub window, the players with the fewest minutes so far go on. Ties are broken in favour of anyone who sat out the previous block (so nobody sits out twice in a row where it can be avoided) and, if switched on, anyone short on season minutes. When the squad size doesn't divide evenly, some players will get one block more than others — the summary shows the spread.

    Season suggestions compare each player's minutes with their fair share of the matches they were available for.
